Privacy Policy

In short: Listok does not collect your data. Everything you log stays on your device, and we cannot see it.

Effective November 7, 2026. This policy explains how the app "Listok: Cycle Tracker" ("Listok", "the app") handles information. The app is developed and owned by Quantyl Games ("we").

1. What the app stores

  • Diary entries: period days and flow, spotting, period end marks, symptoms, mood, intimacy marks (protected/unprotected), notes.
  • Settings: typical cycle and period length, luteal phase length, your name and your sprout's name (if you entered them), reminder preferences, Face ID and Health toggles, the chosen app icon.
  • App state: achievements, the sprout's growth and outfit, and a cache used by the widgets.

You enter this information yourself. The app does not request your location, contacts, photos or advertising identifier, and does not create an account.

2. Where data is stored

Only on your device, in the app's protected container (shared with Listok's own widgets). The app does not connect to the internet, has no server, and does not send data to us or to anyone else. iOS encrypts the data while your device is locked. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, the app's data may be included in that system backup, which is controlled by Apple and by you, not by us.

3. Apple Health (HealthKit)

  • The integration is off by default and only turned on by you in the app's Profile; access is granted in the iOS permission sheet and can be revoked in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.
  • If you turn on saving, Listok writes your logs to Health: menstrual flow, intermenstrual bleeding, symptoms and sexual activity.
  • If you run an import, Listok reads menstrual data from Health to add days that are missing in your diary.
  • HealthKit data is never used for advertising, marketing or profiling, is never sold or shared with third parties, and never leaves your device through Listok. Listok does not modify or delete data written by other apps.

4. Notifications and Face ID

Reminders are scheduled and delivered locally, with no push servers; by default they are discreet, without the words "period" or "ovulation" on the Lock Screen. Face ID uses the iOS system mechanism: biometric data never leaves the device's secure hardware, and the app only receives the result of the check.

5. Backups

You can manually export a backup file (JSON) and restore it. The file is saved wherever you choose (for example, Files or iCloud Drive), and from then on you are responsible for keeping it. It contains your entries and settings, so please store it with care.

6. What we don't do

  • We do not collect or receive any data — personal or anonymous.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking, third-party SDKs or third-party crash reporting.
  • We do not sell or share data — we simply don't have it.

Apple may provide developers with aggregated App Store statistics and crash reports from users who chose to share them with developers in iOS settings; these never include your diary entries.

7. Deleting your data

Profile → Delete All Data erases entries, settings, achievements, the widget cache and scheduled reminders, and — if you choose — the records Listok saved to Health. Deleting the app also removes its data from your device. Because we do not store your data, there is nothing to delete on our side.

8. Children

The app is not specifically directed to children and does not collect data from users of any age.

9. Your rights

All data is under your control: you can view, edit, export and delete it in the app. If you have privacy questions, including under the GDPR or other applicable laws, please contact us.

10. Changes to this policy

If we ever change how data is handled (for example, by adding a feature that needs the network), we will update this page and the date above before such a version is released, and describe the change in the release notes.

FAQ

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen → Edit → Add Widget → find Listok. For the Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen → Customize → Lock Screen → Add Widgets. Widgets refresh after every log and at midnight.

How do I use Siri and Shortcuts?

Try "What cycle day is it in Listok", "When is my period in Listok" or "Period started in Listok". All Listok actions are listed in the Shortcuts app under Listok. You can also add New Cycle, Intimacy and Open Listok buttons to Control Center.

How do I turn on Face ID?

Profile → Protection → Face ID. Listok locks when you leave the app, and its preview is blurred in the app switcher.

How does Apple Health work?

Profile → Apple Health. "Save to Health" stores your new logs. "Import history from Health" adds days that are missing in Listok — a way to bring your history over from another app that syncs with Health. Existing logs are not overwritten, and Listok never touches other apps' records.

How do I back up and restore?

Profile → Backup → Export, and choose a location (for example, iCloud Drive). To restore, tap Import and pick the file. Restoring replaces your current data — Listok will ask you to confirm.

How do I delete all data?

Profile → Delete All Data. Entries, settings, achievements, widget cache and reminders are erased; optionally also Listok's records in Health. This can't be undone, so you may want to save a backup first.

Why does the estimate change?

Estimates are based on your own past logs: the average length of your recent cycles and periods. Until there's enough data, your settings are used. If your period is late, Listok gently shifts the estimate. It's a guide, not a promise.

Can I use Listok for contraception?

No. Listok is not a method of contraception and does not diagnose any condition. The fertile window in the app is an approximate estimate. For health or contraception questions, please talk to a doctor.

What makes the sprout grow?

Days of self-care — days when you log something: your period, symptoms, mood or a note. Missed days are fine: the sprout never shrinks. Its mood follows your cycle phase.

Does Listok send my data anywhere?

No. The app never connects to the internet and does not collect data. See the privacy policy for details.

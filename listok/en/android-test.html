Listok for Android

Thank you for helping! Listok for Android is in closed testing on Google Play. Joining takes a couple of minutes — all you need is an Android phone with a Google account.

  1. Join the testers group

    Open the link and tap "Join group". Sign in with the same Google account you use for the Play Store on your phone. After joining you'll see an empty group page — that's expected: there are no discussions in the group, just come back here and go to step 2.

    Join the group

  2. Become a tester

    Open the testing page and tap "Become a tester". If the page says the app isn't available, check that you're signed in with the same account as in step 1.

    Become a tester

  3. Install Listok from Google Play

    From the same page, go to Google Play, or open the link below on your phone and tap "Install". Sometimes the app takes a while to appear — wait up to an hour and refresh the page.

    Open in Google Play

  4. Keep it for 14 days

    Google releases an app to everyone only after testers have used it for at least two weeks. It helps a lot if you keep Listok on your phone for at least 14 days and open it now and then.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The test version is free, and updates arrive through Google Play as usual.

What about my data?

Same as on iPhone: your diary stays on your phone and we collect nothing. See the privacy policy for details.

Found a bug or have an idea?

Write to info@lapka.kz or leave private feedback on Listok's Google Play page — only we can see test feedback. A screenshot and your phone model help a lot.

How do I leave the test?

Open the page from step 2, tap "Leave the program", then uninstall the app. Please, not before 14 days 🙂

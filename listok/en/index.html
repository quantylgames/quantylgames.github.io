What Listok does

What matters, on one screen

Cycle day, phase and a gentle estimate. The week opens into a month, and All Months shows the whole year.

Log in two taps

"Started" and "Ended" on the Today screen. Symptoms, mood, intimacy and a note live in the day sheet.

A sprout that grows with you

Its mood follows your phase; it grows from your days of self-care. Six stages and a wardrobe unlocked by achievements.

Achievements without guilt

We celebrate the habit of caring for yourself — never your body or a "perfect" cycle. Nothing earned is taken away.

Widgets, Siri, Control Center

Your cycle day on the Home and Lock Screen, a New Cycle button in Control Center, "What cycle day is it in Listok?".

Private by default

No sign-up, analytics or ads; your diary stays on your iPhone. Face ID, file backups, Apple Health and a partner — only if you want them.

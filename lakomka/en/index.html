What Lakomka does

Two columns

Ingredients with amounts on the left, steps on the right — side by side on iPad and in landscape, like a real cookbook.

Dictation

Say the recipe out loud — Lakomka recognizes speech on the device and splits it into ingredients and steps with temperature and time.

Cooking mode

Large steps, timers right from a step, several timers at once. The current step and countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Thermomix TM6 steps

Time, temperature, speed, reverse and modes shown as clear icons, like on the machine's display.

Themes and watercolor

Several themes and watercolor illustrations matched to each dish automatically. Or your own photo.

PDF with real text

One recipe or the whole book with a cover and contents. The text is selectable — great for printing and gifts.

Read-only

Pin the book so a stray tap with wet hands can't change anything.

Private

No sign-up, ads or analytics. Recipes stay on your device and in your personal iCloud. We never see them.

Lakomka Premium

Viewing recipes, search, cooking mode with timers and Live Activities, and iCloud sync are always available. Lakomka Premium unlocks creating and editing recipes, dictation, templates, PDF export and illustrations.

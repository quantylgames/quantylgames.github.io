Privacy Policy

In short: we do not collect your data. Your recipes stay on your device and in your personal iCloud.

1. Who we are

Lakomka for iPhone and iPad is developed by Quantyl Games. Contact: info@lapka.kz.

2. What data we collect

None. Lakomka has no servers of its own, no accounts, analytics, ads or third-party SDKs. We do not receive your recipes, photos, voice recordings or usage information. The App Store listing says "Data Not Collected".

3. Where recipes are stored

  • On your device — in the app's storage.
  • In your iCloud — if you are signed in and keep sync on (Book settings → iCloud). The data lives in the private CloudKit database of your Apple ID and is available only to your devices. The developer has no access to it. iCloud storage is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

4. Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used only for recipe dictation and only after you tap the record button. Speech is recognized on the device; audio is not stored or sent anywhere. Splitting the text into ingredients and steps also happens on the device (Apple Foundation Models or built-in rules).

5. Photos and illustrations

If you replace an illustration with your photo, the app receives only the selected photo through the system photo picker, without access to your whole library. Image Playground illustrations are created on the device.

6. Notifications and Live Activities

Cooking timers use local notifications and Live Activities created on the device, without push servers.

7. Purchases

The Lakomka Premium subscription is sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details; the app only asks Apple whether the subscription is active.

8. PDF export

PDFs are created on the device. You decide where to send them through the system Share menu.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data about anyone, including children.

10. Deleting data

Delete recipes in the app or delete the app. Data in iCloud can be removed in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Lakomka.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date below. Material changes would also be reflected in the App Store listing.

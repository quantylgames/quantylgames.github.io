FAQ

How do I sync recipes between iPhone and iPad?

Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple ID on both devices and keep "iCloud sync" on in Book settings (the "…" menu in the recipe list). Changes usually appear on the other device within seconds to minutes. Turning sync on or off takes effect after restarting the app.

Recipes don't appear on my other device
  • Check that both devices use the same Apple ID and iCloud Drive is on.
  • Settings → your name → iCloud → Apps Using iCloud → Lakomka is on.
  • You are online and have free iCloud storage.
  • Open Lakomka on both devices and wait a couple of minutes.

How do I cancel the subscription?

Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Lakomka Premium → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period, otherwise it renews. Premium keeps working until the end of the paid period, and your recipes stay with you.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID, open the Premium screen in Lakomka and tap "Restore Purchases".

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. We have no access to payments.

How do I dictate a recipe?

Open a recipe → Edit → Dictate (or "+" → a template → Dictate). Tap the microphone and say the title, the ingredients and then the steps (in Russian). Tap Parse, check the result and tap Fill recipe. Recognition runs on the device, even offline. If the microphone doesn't work, check Settings → Lakomka → Microphone and Speech Recognition.

Does Lakomka work with Thermomix?

You can record Thermomix TM6 steps in recipes: time, temperature, speed, reverse and modes, shown as icons. Lakomka does not connect to the machine and is not affiliated with Vorwerk; it's a convenient way to note the settings you set on the appliance yourself.

The timer doesn't ring when the app is in the background

Allow notifications: Settings → Lakomka → Notifications. Live Activities are enabled there too. The timer keeps counting even if the app is closed.

How do I protect the book from accidental edits?

The "…" menu in the recipe list → Pin (read-only). Edit and delete buttons are hidden. Unpin there too, with a confirmation.

Does Lakomka send my data anywhere?

No. We collect no data. Recipes stay on your device and in your personal iCloud. See the privacy policy.

2. Free features

Always available without a subscription: viewing your recipes and the built-in samples, search, cooking mode with timers and Live Activities, iCloud sync, read-only mode.

The subscription unlocks creating and editing recipes, dictation, templates, PDF export, illustrations and Image Playground. Prices may differ in other countries — the exact price in your currency is shown in the app before purchase.

  • The free week is available once to new subscribers. Any unused part of the trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.

  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Bought on another device? Tap "Restore Purchases" in the app (same Apple ID).

One-time purchase: Premium Forever

The same Premium features can be bought once — 14,990 ₸ (Kazakhstan; prices may differ in other countries, the exact price is shown in the app before purchase).

  • It is a one-time payment, not a subscription: no trial, no auto-renewal, nothing to cancel.
  • Premium stays with you permanently on all devices signed in with your Apple ID. On a new device tap "Restore Purchases".
  • Buying Premium Forever does not cancel an existing subscription automatically. If you have one, turn off its auto-renewal in Settings → your name → Subscriptions — the app reminds you about it in the book settings.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. After a refund, Premium from this purchase is turned off.

4. When the subscription ends

Your recipes stay: you can view, search and cook from them. Creating, editing, dictation and export become available again after you renew or buy Premium Forever. If you already own Premium Forever, the end of a subscription changes nothing.

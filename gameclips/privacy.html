Privacy Policy

GameClips · effective 14 November 2026

This policy explains what information the desktop app GameClips (the “App”) handles. The App is made by Quantyl Games (“we”). Contact: support@quantyl.kz.

The short version

  • The App runs on your computer. We run no servers and do not collect, store or sell your personal data.
  • Your recordings, clips and settings stay on your computer.
  • A clip leaves your computer only when you press “Publish”, and it goes only to the platform you chose (TikTok, YouTube or Instagram).
  • Access tokens of connected accounts are stored only on your computer, in a file readable only by your user.

1. Data stored on your computer

Recordings you open, the clips the App makes, your edits, templates, settings and the publication log (which clip went where and its statistics) are stored in local files on your computer. They are not sent to us.

2. Connected accounts (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram)

When you connect an account, you sign in on the platform’s own page and approve the requested permissions. The App receives an access token from the platform and stores it locally (on macOS in ~/Library/Application Support/GameClips, file permissions 600). The App does not see your password.

With TikTok, the App uses these permissions:

  • user.info.basic — to show which account is connected (display name, open ID);
  • video.upload and video.publish — to upload the clip you chose as a draft or a post, with the caption and privacy level you set;
  • video.list — to show view, like, comment and share counts of clips you published with the App.

The App uses this data only to provide these functions to you, on your computer. Data is exchanged directly between your computer and the platform; it does not pass through us. The platforms process it under their own policies, for example the TikTok Privacy Policy.

3. Optional AI caption suggestions

If you press “Suggest with Claude”, the App extracts a few still frames from the clip and sends them, together with the clip’s text hook and game name, to Anthropic’s Claude through the Claude Code app signed in on your computer, to suggest a caption and hashtags. This happens only when you press the button. Anthropic processes this data under its own privacy policy.

4. Disconnecting and deleting data

“Disconnect” in the App deletes the stored tokens. You can also revoke the App’s access in your TikTok, Google or Instagram account settings. Deleting the App’s folders removes all local data.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13.

6. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.

Политика конфиденциальности

GameClips · действует с 14 ноября 2026

Политика описывает, какие данные обрабатывает приложение для компьютера GameClips. Разработчик — Quantyl Games. Контакт: support@quantyl.kz.

  • Приложение работает на вашем компьютере. У нас нет серверов, мы не собираем, не храним и не продаём ваши персональные данные.
  • Записи, ролики и настройки остаются на вашем компьютере.
  • Ролик уходит с компьютера только по нажатию «Опубликовать» и только на выбранную площадку (TikTok, YouTube или Instagram).
  • Токены доступа подключённых аккаунтов хранятся только локально (~/Library/Application Support/GameClips, права 600). Пароль приложение не видит.
  • Разрешения TikTok: user.info.basic — показать подключённый аккаунт; video.upload и video.publish — загрузить выбранный ролик черновиком или постом; video.list — показать статистику роликов, опубликованных через приложение.
  • По кнопке «Придумать с Claude» несколько кадров ролика, хук и название игры отправляются в Claude (Anthropic) через Claude Code на вашем компьютере, чтобы предложить подпись и хештеги.
  • «Отключить» удаляет токены; доступ также можно отозвать в настройках аккаунта площадки.
